How it works

From a long document to a short list of what matters.

  1. Add the document

    Upload a PDF or text file, or paste the text. ClauseGuard suggests what kind of document it is; you can change it.

  2. It is read in pieces

    The text is cleaned and split into sentences or paragraphs: the same units the model was trained on.

  3. Each piece is classified

    A transformer model fine-tuned for that document type flags the clauses that match a known risk category.

  4. You get a clear report

    A 0–100 risk score, the flagged clauses highlighted in the text, and a plain-language explanation of each.
