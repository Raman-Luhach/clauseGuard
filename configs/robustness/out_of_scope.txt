The city council approved a plan on Tuesday to build two new parks and extend the cycle lane along the river. Construction is due to begin next spring and should take about eighteen months.

A new exhibition of nineteenth-century landscape paintings opens at the national gallery next month. The collection includes several works that have never been shown in public, on loan from private owners.

Researchers at the university have developed a cheaper way to recycle plastic bottles into clothing fibres. The team hopes to work with manufacturers to test the process at a larger scale within two years.

Local bakeries report strong demand for seasonal bread, and several have extended their opening hours. One owner said the new recipe uses flour from farms within thirty miles of the shop.
